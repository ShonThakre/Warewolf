# How to play

Werewolf is a game of hidden roles. Most players are villagers; a few are wolves pretending to be villagers. Talk, watch, and vote to find them.

The same text is in the app at **How to Play** (`/how-to-play`). Seat counts, timers, and starting roles on that page are read from the game rules, so they stay current. If this page and the app disagree, the app is right.

## The goal

- **Village:** find the wolves and vote every one of them out.
- **Wolves:** stay hidden, and remove villagers until there are as many wolves as everyone else.
- **Solo roles:** a few roles, such as the Jester, have a goal of their own. Their card says how they win.

## One round

1. **Night.** Everyone's eyes are closed. Roles with a power pick a player in secret. Wolves choose who to attack.
2. **Morning.** The game shows who was eliminated in the night, if anyone. Private results go only to the player who earned them.
3. **Day.** Living players talk in the discussion. Share what you know, or bluff.
4. **Vote.** Each living player votes for someone, or abstains. The most votes is eliminated. A tie gets one revote between the tied players. A second tie eliminates nobody.

Then the next night begins. The game ends as soon as one side has won.

## Your role card

- **Read it alone.** Your card shows your team, your power, and how you win. Nobody else can see it. Use **Hide role** when someone is looking.
- **Use your power.** At night, choose a player and press **Submit**. You can change your choice until the host closes the night.
- **If you are eliminated,** you can still read the discussion, but you cannot talk, vote, or act.

## The host

- **Sets up the room.** Creates the game, picks the mode, and shares the room code. The game starts once every seat is filled.
- **Moves the game on.** Closes each phase with the button at the bottom of the screen, unless the mode runs on timers.
- **Sees everything.** The host dashboard shows every role and every night choice. The host also plays a seat, so play fair.

## Game modes

### Infection Edition: 20 players, host-paced

The main game. One hidden Alpha Wolf grows the pack by turning villagers into wolves.

- Each night the Alpha Wolf can infect one player. That player becomes a wolf, quietly. It can happen up to 4 times.
- Wolves do not know who the other wolves are.
- The host picks the night's attack from the names the wolves chose. The Doctor is told who will be attacked and can save them.
- Seekers check one player a night: wolf or not. A Seeker can also read their result out to the room during the day. The Guard protects one player. The Witch has one save and one poison for the whole game.

Starting roles: **Village:** 14 Villagers, 2 Seekers, Guard, Doctor, Witch. **Wolf:** Alpha Wolf.

### Classic: 6 to 20 players, timed

The best first game. A small pack of wolves who know each other, against a village with a Seeker and a Doctor.

- No infection: the pack never grows.
- Wolves see their teammates and agree on one attack.
- Timers move the game on: 90 seconds of night, 180 of discussion, and 60 to vote. The host can still move on early.

Starting roles (10 players): **Village:** 6 Villagers, Seeker, Doctor. **Wolf:** 2 Pack Wolves.

### Expanded: 10 to 30 players, host-paced

The same game with many more roles to discover.

- Ready-made setups focus on information, protection, or chaos, or seat 24 or 30 players: Balanced Expanded, Investigation Edition, Protection Edition, Chaos, Expanded 24, and Expanded 30.
- Solo roles join. The Jester wins by being voted out, the Assassin by having their mark die, and the Lone Wolf by being the last one standing.
- When a solo player wins, the game can keep going for everyone else.

### Ultimate 20: 20 players, host-paced

Twenty seats, fifteen special roles, and several goals running at once.

- The Alpha Wolf can kill or infect each night. The Shadow Wolf cancels someone's power.
- The Vampire marks players who later join the Vampire team in secret.
- Watch for the Joker, the Fox, the Necromancer, and the Chaos Demon: each wins their own way.
- A Ghost who is eliminated can leave the room one hint. The Alchemist can silence a player for a day.

The full Ultimate 20 rules are in [ultimate-20-rules.md](./ultimate-20-rules.md).

### Custom: 6 to 30 players

The host picks every role and every rule.

- Choose any roles the catalog allows.
- The setup is checked before the game starts. It needs at least one wolf.
- Start from Classic and change one thing at a time.
